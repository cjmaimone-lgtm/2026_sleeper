How this works

Each team's best possible lineup is set every remaining week from Sleeper's player projections, scored with this league's rules (no injuries, trades, or pickups assumed). Real scores have landed about ±23.4 points from projections so far this season, so every simulated week adds that much randomness. Each week a team plays its opponent and the league median. Ties in the standings go to points scored. The playoff bracket is fixed: 1 vs. winner of 4/5, winner of 2/7 vs. winner of 3/6.
